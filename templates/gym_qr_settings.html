How Members Use This
No extra setup on your end — this works with the existing attendance system.
1 Member opens the EnterGYM app and taps Scan QR Code on the Attendance page.
2 They point their camera at this QR — displayed at your reception.
3 Attendance is marked instantly if their membership is active and they're enrolled in this gym.
4 Expired or unenrolled scans show the member a popup — and appear in your dashboard alerts below.
