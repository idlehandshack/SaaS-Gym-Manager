Data Deletion Instructions

Delete Your Data

You're in control of your data. This page explains what we delete, how long it takes, and how to submit a request — including for data connected through WhatsApp.

Effective: 29 July 2026
Arrow SoftTech
The short version: Email us at shiekazmatulla@gmail.com with your name, gym name, and phone number. We verify the request and permanently delete your personal data within 30 days, except records we're legally required to keep (like invoices).
1

What Data We Hold

Depending on how you use EnterGYM (as a gym owner, staff member, or gym member), we may hold the following categories of personal data:

  • Name, email address, and phone number
  • Gym membership details — plan, join date, renewal history
  • Attendance records, including GPS check-in and AI Face Attendance data
  • Payment and billing history, including GST invoices
  • WhatsApp message logs sent via our WhatsApp Cloud API integration (e.g. payment reminders, expiry alerts)
  • Profile photos uploaded to your gym's dashboard
2

How to Request Deletion

Send us an email with the details below. This lets us locate your record and confirm it's really you before we delete anything.

Submit a Deletion Request

Include the following in your email so we can process it without back-and-forth:

  • Your full name
  • Your email address
  • Your gym name (if applicable)
  • The phone number associated with your account
  • A brief description of your deletion request

4

Data Shared via WhatsApp

EnterGYM uses the WhatsApp Cloud API (provided by Meta) to send membership and payment reminders on behalf of gym owners. When you interact with a gym through WhatsApp:

  • Your phone number and message content are processed by Meta to deliver the message
  • We store a copy of message logs (e.g. delivery status, message ID) to help gym owners confirm reminders were sent
  • We do not use your WhatsApp data for advertising or share it with any third party beyond Meta's role as the messaging provider
Note: Deleting your data from EnterGYM removes it from our systems, but does not delete your WhatsApp account or messages stored on your own device. If you'd also like to manage what Meta/WhatsApp itself retains, you can do so directly through your WhatsApp app settings or Meta's Accounts Center.
5

What We're Required to Keep

Some records can't be deleted immediately, even on request, because Indian law requires us to retain them:

GST invoices and tax-related billing records are retained for the period required under Indian tax law, even after a deletion request is processed. Records needed for an active legal dispute or fraud investigation may also be retained until resolved.

All other personal data — profile details, attendance history, face recognition data, and message logs — is fully and permanently deleted once your request is verified.
