Got questions? We've got answers. Everything you need to know about EnterGYM.

EnterGYM is an all-in-one gym management software designed for gyms and fitness centers. It helps you manage members, memberships, attendance, payments, invoices, trainers, expenses, notifications, and everyday gym operations from one platform.

Managing a gym using paper registers, spreadsheets, and separate payment records can make it difficult to track members, attendance, pending payments, and membership expiry. EnterGYM brings these operations together in one dashboard so you can spend less time on administration and more time growing your gym.

EnterGYM includes member management, membership plans, attendance tracking, payment and invoice management, trainer management, expense tracking, announcements, automated membership expiry notifications, member portals, QR attendance, and AI face attendance support. All Premium+ features are included in the subscription.

Yes. EnterGYM can send membership expiry notifications to members through supported push notification channels. This helps members stay informed about their membership validity and upcoming renewal dates, reducing the chance of missed renewals.

EnterGYM currently offers fixed subscription plans based on your gym's active member capacity. The Starter plan is ₹399/month for up to 75 members, Growth is ₹549/month for up to 120 members, and Professional is ₹899/month for up to 250 members. Multi-branch gyms can contact us for custom Chain Pro pricing.

Yes. EnterGYM provides a mobile-friendly member experience along with an Android app experience. Members can access their gym information, membership details, attendance features, and other available member services directly from their phone.

Yes. You can move to a higher subscription plan when your gym grows and requires a larger member capacity. Contact our team through WhatsApp and we will help you upgrade your plan.

Members can view their membership information, check attendance history, access payment and receipt information, receive membership notifications, and use the attendance features provided by their gym. The available features depend on the services enabled by the gym.

Yes. New gyms can start with a 7-day free trial with access to Premium+ features for up to 40 members. No payment card is required to start the trial. You can upgrade to a paid subscription whenever you are ready.

Yes. EnterGYM supports member notifications for important gym updates such as membership expiry reminders and announcements. Gyms can use the notification system to keep members informed without relying entirely on manual communication.

EnterGYM supports multiple attendance methods, including QR code attendance, GPS-based attendance, manual staff check-in, and AI-powered face recognition attendance. Attendance records are timestamped and connected to the member's profile so gym staff can easily review attendance history.

EnterGYM supports role-based access for Super Admin, Gym Owner, Trainer, and Receptionist. Each role can be given access to the functions required for their responsibilities, helping gym owners keep operational data organized and controlled.

Yes. EnterGYM is built as a multi-tenant platform and supports multi-branch gym operations. Each gym or branch can have its own members, staff, attendance, payments, settings, and operational data while owners can manage their business from a centralized platform.

Yes. EnterGYM provides payment and invoice management for member transactions and also supports gym expense tracking. This gives gym owners better visibility into their collections, expenses, and overall financial performance.

Getting started is simple. Start the 7-day free trial, configure your gym information, add your staff and members, and begin managing your gym from the EnterGYM dashboard. If you need help choosing a plan or setting up your gym, contact our team on WhatsApp.

Yes. EnterGYM uses a multi-tenant architecture where each gym's operational data is scoped to its own gym account. Members, attendance, payments, staff, settings, and other gym information are kept within the appropriate gym context.
